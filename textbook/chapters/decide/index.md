# Part V · Decide

**Decision rules, optimisation, simulation, and experimentation.**

Prediction alone is not enough. A model that predicts churn probability with 95% accuracy is worthless unless it connects to a decision: *who do we call, how much do we offer, and when?* The Decide stage is where data science becomes decision science.

This part covers 12 nodes that translate model outputs and analytical findings into structured decisions — through rule engines, optimisation, scenario simulation, controlled experiments, and portfolio optimisation.

## From Prediction to Action

The gap between prediction and decision is where most analytics initiatives fail. Common failure modes:

- **No decision framework** — the model is built but nobody knows how to act on it
- **Ignoring uncertainty** — decisions are made on point estimates without considering the range of outcomes
- **No experimentation** — interventions are deployed without measuring whether they actually work
- **Optimising the wrong objective** — maximising model accuracy instead of business value

The Decide nodes are designed to close this gap.
