# Part VI · Explain

**Causal inference, attribution, impact measurement, and interpretation.**

Correlation is not causation — and in business, causation is what matters. Did our marketing campaign cause the increase in sales, or was it the product launch? Does the drug cause recovery, or do healthier patients choose to take it? Did the new pricing policy cause customer churn, or would they have churned anyway?

The Explain stage is where data science gets rigorous about cause and effect. It covers 23 nodes spanning feature importance, causal inference, quasi-experimental methods, mediation analysis, and counterfactual reasoning.

## The Ladder of Causation

Following Judea Pearl's framework, there are three levels of causal reasoning:

1. **Association** — *seeing*: "What is the correlation between X and Y?" (Explore / Understand)
2. **Intervention** — *doing*: "What happens to Y if I change X?" (Explain)
3. **Counterfactual** — *imagining*: "What would Y have been if X had been different?" (Explain)

Most of the Explain nodes operate at levels 2 and 3.
