# Part III · Understand

**Statistical analysis, profiling, hypothesis testing, and pattern discovery.**

The Understand stage is where data starts to reveal its secrets. Before building predictive models, good data science requires deep understanding: What does the data look like? What relationships exist? Are the patterns statistically significant? Are there anomalies, drift, or structural changes?

This part covers 31 nodes spanning descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, clustering, time series decomposition, network analysis, and more.

## The Scientific Method in Practice

Every chapter in this part embodies the scientific method:
1. **Formulate a question** — what are we trying to understand?
2. **State a hypothesis** — what do we expect to find?
3. **Collect evidence** — run the analysis
4. **Evaluate significance** — is the finding real or noise?
5. **Draw conclusions** — what does this mean for the business?
