# Part VII · Augment

**Large language models, embeddings, NLP, and generative AI.**

The Augment stage brings the power of modern AI — large language models, semantic embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation, and computer vision — into structured data science workflows. This is where unstructured data (text, images, documents) becomes structured signal, and where AI reasoning is applied at scale.

This part covers 14 nodes spanning the full stack of applied AI: from document understanding and sentiment analysis to vector search, topic modelling, LLM-powered transformation, and fine-tuning.

## The Augment Philosophy

The Augment nodes are designed around a key insight: **unstructured data is everywhere in business, and it contains enormous signal that traditional data science ignores.** Customer reviews, support tickets, contracts, emails, images, audio transcripts — all of this can be turned into structured features using the Augment nodes, then fed into prediction and decision models downstream.
